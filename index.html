Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding wood quality, custom ordering, and delivery policy.

What wood species do you use for SS Furniture items?
We use 100% seasoned Grade-A Teak wood, Indian Sheesham, and imported American Walnut. All wood is kiln-dried to eliminate moisture and prevent warping or pest infestation.
How long does custom furniture crafting take?
Bespoke custom furniture pieces typically require 14 to 21 business days for precision crafting, polishing, and quality audit before dispatch.
Do you provide free home delivery and assembly?
Yes! We offer free insured white-glove home delivery and professional on-site assembly for all orders within our primary service zones.
What warranty is included with my furniture purchase?
Every piece comes with a 10-year structural warranty covering wood frame integrity, anti-termite protection, and joinery durability.
