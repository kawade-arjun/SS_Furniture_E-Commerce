Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our solid wood, custom orders, delivery, and warranty policies.

What wood species do you use for SS Furniture items?
We use 100% seasoned Grade-A Teak wood, Indian Sheesham, and imported American Walnut. All wood is kiln-dried for 6 weeks to eliminate moisture and prevent warping, cracking, or pest infestation over decades.
How long does custom furniture crafting take?
Custom furniture orders take between 14 to 21 business days. This timeframe allows our master carpenters to execute precision joinery, hand sanding, lacquer polishing, and thorough quality audits.
Do you provide free home delivery and assembly?
Yes! We provide free insured white-glove home delivery and professional on-site assembly for all orders within our primary service locations.
What warranty is included with my furniture purchase?
Every item includes a 10-year structural warranty covering solid wood frames, anti-termite treatment, and joinery integrity.
Can I choose custom fabric textures and colors?
Absolutely. We carry over 200+ physical upholstery swatches in our showroom including top-grain Italian leather, stain-resistant suede, plush velvet, and woven cotton linen.
